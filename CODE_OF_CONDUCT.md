# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in KiroCrew a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Assume good faith while discussing technical disagreements.
- Focus feedback on the work and its impact, not on individuals.
- Protect private information, credentials, and security-sensitive details.
- Follow the project's security and contribution guidelines.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, and any other conduct that would make a reasonable participant feel unsafe are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's [private security contact](https://github.com/jersonmartinez/kiro-crew/security/advisories/new) or another private contact configured by the maintainers. Do not open a public issue when the report contains personal information, credentials, or security-sensitive details.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove comments, restrict participation, or take other action appropriate to the situation.

## Scope and enforcement

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and public interactions when someone is representing KiroCrew or its community. Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards fairly and consistently.
